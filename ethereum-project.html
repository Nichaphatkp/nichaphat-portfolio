01

Project Overview

This project studies Ethereum price forecasting and investment-risk evaluation using quantitative financial models. Ethereum was selected because cryptocurrency prices exhibit high volatility, fat-tail behavior and sudden price movements.

The project compares Geometric Brownian Motion (GBM) with Merton Jump Diffusion (MJD) to determine how effectively each model represents Ethereum price behavior, forecasting performance and downside risk.

01

Price Forecasting

Forecasted Ethereum price behavior using historical ETH/USD market data.

02

Model Comparison

Compared GBM and Merton Jump Diffusion under highly volatile market conditions.

03

Risk Evaluation

Evaluated downside and tail risk using Value at Risk and Expected Shortfall.

02

Data, Models & Methodology

Daily ETH/USD closing-price data from January 2023 to December 2024 were transformed into daily returns and analyzed using statistical methods.

Parameters were estimated for both GBM and MJD, followed by Monte Carlo simulation to study price behavior, return distributions and investment risk.

MODEL 01

Geometric Brownian Motion

Represents continuous asset-price movements using drift and volatility components.

MODEL 02

Merton Jump Diffusion

Extends GBM by including sudden price jumps to better represent extreme market behavior.

SIMULATION

Monte Carlo Simulation

Generated 1,000,000 simulated observations for return-distribution and risk analysis.

Results & Project Outcome

Sudden Price Jumps

✦
KEY RESULT

Merton Jump Diffusion better represented Ethereum's extreme market behavior.

The analysis showed that GBM tends to underestimate extreme cryptocurrency risk, while MJD provides a more realistic representation of sudden price movements and tail-risk behavior.

This project strengthened my experience in quantitative modeling, statistical analysis, Monte Carlo simulation and financial-risk evaluation.
