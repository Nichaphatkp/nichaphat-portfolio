01

Project Overview

Ethereum is a highly volatile digital asset whose price can change rapidly due to market conditions and unexpected events. This project studies whether stochastic mathematical models can effectively forecast Ethereum prices and evaluate extreme investment risk.

01

Price Forecasting

Analyze historical ETH/USD data and compare forecasting performance between mathematical models.

02

Risk Evaluation

Evaluate downside investment risk using Value at Risk and Expected Shortfall.

03

Model Comparison

Compare Geometric Brownian Motion with Merton Jump Diffusion for volatile cryptocurrency behavior.

02

Data & Methodology

DATASET

ETH/USD Daily Closing Prices

Historical daily Ethereum closing-price data covering January 2023 to December 2024.

MODEL 01

Geometric Brownian Motion

Traditional stochastic model used to represent continuous asset-price movements.

MODEL 02

Merton Jump Diffusion

Extends GBM by incorporating sudden price jumps, allowing extreme cryptocurrency movements to be modeled more realistically.

Forecasting Performance

Forecasting performance was evaluated using RMSE and MAE. The Merton Jump Diffusion model produced slightly lower forecasting errors than the standard GBM model.

MODEL RMSE MAE
Geometric Brownian Motion 103.68 75.044
Merton Jump Diffusion 103.16 75.028
06

Risk Analysis

VALUE AT RISK

Downside Risk

Value at Risk was used to estimate the potential loss threshold at a selected confidence level.

EXPECTED SHORTFALL

Extreme Loss

Expected Shortfall was used to measure average losses beyond the VaR threshold.

TAIL RISK

Extreme Market Behavior

MJD represented fat-tail behavior and sudden Ethereum price jumps more effectively than GBM.

07

Project Outcome

✦
KEY FINDING

Merton Jump Diffusion provides a more realistic representation of Ethereum market risk.

Ethereum returns exhibit high volatility, fat-tail behavior and sudden price jumps. The analysis found that MJD was more suitable than standard GBM for representing these extreme movements and also produced slightly lower forecasting errors.
