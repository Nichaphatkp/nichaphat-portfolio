01

Project Overview

This project develops a data-driven decision-support system for analyzing and forecasting Apple (AAPL) stock prices using historical market data.

Machine Learning was applied to historical price information to forecast next-day closing prices, identify market trends and transform raw market data into useful analytical insights.

01

Price Forecasting

Predicts next-day AAPL closing prices using historical market information.

02

Trend Analysis

Uses moving averages and market indicators to analyze price direction.

03

Risk Monitoring

Uses volatility information to identify changing market-risk conditions.

02

Data & Feature Engineering

The analysis uses historical Apple stock market data from 1980–2025, including Open, High, Low, Close, Adjusted Close and Volume.

Feature engineering was performed to capture short-term trends, medium-term market behavior and changing levels of volatility.

MARKET DATA

OHLC & Volume

Open, High, Low, Close, Adjusted Close and Volume were used as the analytical foundation.

FEATURES

Technical Indicators

Generated MA7, MA30, Daily Return and Rolling Volatility.

TARGET

Next-Day Close

Next-day closing price was defined as the primary regression target.

Model Performance & Key Insights

Linear Regression was selected as the primary forecasting model and evaluated using chronological 80/20 time-series validation to reduce data leakage.
