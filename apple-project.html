01

Project Overview

The project develops an intelligent system to analyze and forecast Apple stock prices using historical market data. Machine Learning is applied to transform raw technical data into decision-support insights.

01

Price Forecasting

Predict next-day AAPL closing prices using historical market variables.

02

Trend Detection

Detect potential uptrend opportunities and support market-entry decisions.

03

Risk Management

Analyze volatility and market regimes to support dynamic risk-control strategies.

02

Data & Preparation

DATASET

Apple Stock Data

Daily AAPL time-series data spanning more than 45 years from 1980 to 2025.

BASE FEATURES

OHLC & Volume

Open, High, Low, Close, Adjusted Close and Volume were used as the analytical foundation.

TARGET

Next-Day Close

Next_Close was defined as the primary regression target for price prediction.

03

Feature Engineering

MA7 MA30 Daily Return Rolling Volatility OHLC Volume Market Regimes

Short- and medium-term moving averages were generated together with return and volatility metrics to capture price trends and changing market-risk conditions.

RMSE ±$2.17

The model was validated using a strict chronological 80/20 Time-Series Split, training on historical data and testing on future observations.
